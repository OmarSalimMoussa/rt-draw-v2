Each building block of the v2 canvas, tested on its own before anything is assembled. None of these pages call a model.

1. Renderer

A scene of layered shapes drawn in the doodle style: text inside shapes, picture placeholders, fade, z order, groups, optional grid and id tags.

2. Ops stream

Op lines streamed in token-sized chunks and applied the moment each line completes. Prose, unknown ids and cut-off lines show up as errors.

3. Ink

Draw with the pen: smooth strokes with a time on every point, ready to be matched to your words.

4. Hit-test

Draw over a page and see which shapes each stroke touches, encloses, starts on and ends on.

5. Marks

Strokes grouped into marks and read as pointing (loop, arrow, tap, line, scribble) or sketch. Correct the readings, save the set, rerun it after changing the rules.

6. Arrow or not

Draw marks; a local geometry check says arrow or not and finds the tip and tail in under a millisecond. Label them, save the set, rerun it after changing the rules.
