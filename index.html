Each building block of the v2 canvas, tested on its own before anything is assembled. Pages 1-10 call no model (Speech and Ink ↔ words call Scribe, paid, only when you press Talk or Start). Pages 11-15 call Claude on your subscription, only when you press their run button.

1. Renderer

A scene of layered shapes drawn in the doodle style: text inside shapes, picture placeholders, fade, z order, groups, optional grid and id tags.

2. Ops stream

Op lines streamed in token-sized chunks and applied the moment each line completes. Prose, unknown ids and cut-off lines show up as errors.

3. Ink

Draw with the pen: smooth strokes with a time on every point, ready to be matched to your words.

4. Hit-test

Draw over a page and see which shapes each stroke touches, encloses, starts on and ends on.

5. Marks

Strokes grouped into marks and read as pointing (loop, arrow, tap, line, scribble) or sketch. Each mark is also checked for being an arrow, with its tip and tail. Correct the readings, save the set, rerun it after changing the rules.

6. Speech

Talk and get words, each with a time, over the waveform. ElevenLabs Scribe, paid per minute.

7. Ink ↔ words

Talk while you draw (Scribe) and see [ink n] land at the word you were saying. Demo is free.

8. Model screenshot

The picture the model would get at Enter: the canvas with numbered ink, id tags and an optional grid, timed and sized.

9. Coordinates

Click or drag a place and see how each scheme would name it (pixels, grid cells, relative to a shape), and whether it reads back to the same place.

10. Tidy pass

A messy scene snapped, aligned and evened out; before and after, with every move listed.

11. Shape descriptions

Each shape's crop sent to a small vision model for the detailed id map; compare with the descriptions the ops wrote.

12. Reference resolver

"this headline" plus your ink → the shape ids it means. Hand-written cases, scored.

13. Drawing model

One send (words, ink, screenshot, id map) → ops streamed back and applied as they arrive.

14. Picture model

A doodle drawn inside a shape from a few words, side by side for several subjects, timed.

15. Router

Which lane each send takes (fast or full), by rules first, over labeled sends.

16. Board shots

An infinite board of pages: what a model can be shown of it. Views picked for the send and captured side by side (what you see, close-ups of marks, pages you named, the whole board) next to the site map as text, with what each costs. No model call.

17. Camera

"Zoom out", "go to the shop page", "a little to the left": can a model move the camera the way a person means? Cases scored by rules, or type your own and watch it glide. Calls Claude on your subscription.

Evals

Driver

A model plays a user (first-timer with a site in mind, explorer, or breaker): it looks at the screen, talks and scribbles (synthesized, seeded), presses Enter, and rates what it got. Calls Claude on your subscription.
